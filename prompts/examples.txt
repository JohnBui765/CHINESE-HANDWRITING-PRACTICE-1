You are helping a beginner learn Chinese with a handwriting-practice app. The learner is at
{LEVEL}. For EACH word in the list at the end, write ONE example sentence that uses it. The
learner cannot check your work: a flagged sentence (" ?") is better than a silent mistake.

THE SENTENCE
1. Natural and correct: something a native speaker would really say in everyday life. No
   textbook-style or made-up-sounding sentences, and no definitions ("X means ...").
2. Easy: use only words of {LEVEL} or a lower level, plus the words of the list. When you are
   not sure of a word's level, choose a simpler word.
3. Use the word exactly as it is written in the list, in one piece, in its most common
   meaning. Never change, split or replace its characters.
4. Short: 5 to 10 boxes, never more than 12. Each Chinese character is one box, and so is
   each punctuation mark. The app refuses longer sentences.
5. Simplified characters and Chinese punctuation; end with 。？ or ！. Write numbers in
   Chinese characters (三, not 3).

PINYIN (of the whole sentence, as it is said)
6. Tone marks (ā á ǎ à), never tone numbers. The syllables of one word written together
   (péngyou), words separated by spaces, the first word and names capitalised, and the
   sentence's end mark in English form (. ? !).
7. Neutral tones and r-endings as in the dictionary (péngyou, xuésheng, 哪儿 nǎr).
8. Apply these tone changes, and no others:
   a. 3rd + 3rd (the second not neutral): the first becomes 2nd (你好 ní hǎo; 我有 wó yǒu).
      For three or more 3rd tones, work from the smallest meaning unit outward, using the
      tones as already changed: 我也很好 = [我也][很好] → wó yě hén hǎo.
   b. 3rd + neutral tone: usually no change (姐姐 jiějie, 你们 nǐmen); a few words do change
      (小姐 xiáojie, 哪里 náli). If unsure, keep the 3rd tone and add " ?".
   c. 不 bù → bú before a 4th tone (不是, 不客气).
   d. 一 yī → yí before a 4th tone (一个, 一样); yī → yì before a 1st, 2nd or 3rd tone (一天,
      一年, 一起). 一 stays yī as a digit or ordinal, in dates and at the end of a word
      (第一, 一月, 十一).
   e. For 不 and 一, judge by the next syllable's dictionary tone, even when it is said
      neutral (一个 yí ge).
   f. 不 and 一 are neutral inside doubled forms (是不是, 看一看) and in verb + 不 +
      complement (对不起 duìbuqǐ). This overrides c and d.
   g. 七 and 八 never change.

ENGLISH
9. A natural English translation of the sentence: at most 10 words, no notes, no brackets.

OUTPUT: reply with ONLY one code block in exactly this format, nothing before or after it.
- For each word of the list, in the same order: the word alone on its line, exactly as
  given; directly under it, its sentence line:
  = sentence | pinyin as said | English translation
- End the sentence line with " ?" if you are not sure that it is natural and correct.

CHECK
- Every word of the list is there, in order, each with one sentence line.
- Each sentence contains its word unchanged and has at most 12 boxes.
- Each sentence's pinyin has one syllable for each character (哪儿 nǎr: one for two).
